The research problem

A constitution is not only a hierarchy of norms.

Constitutional theory reads a constitution as a static pyramid of sources to be interpreted, or as a legal-historical object to be described case by case. Both capture the consolidated structure and miss what the order demonstrably is in operation.

Emergence & downward causation

A macro-order not present in any single actor or article, which then conditions which micro-behaviours may update next.

Nonlinearity & tipping points

Episodic strain that suddenly becomes self-sustaining — a closure loop that shuts, after which only multi-nodal intervention works.

Path dependence & multiple realisation

Ratchets that do not relax on their own, and the same macro-outcome reached from opposite micro-configurations — structural invariance to content.

The generalisation gap. The project has validated single-domain cycles of exactly this kind. There is no integrated instrument yet that reads a constitutional system as a whole as a complex adaptive system, on a common ontological footing, with a reproducible structural notation and a governed simulation layer that moves from diagnosis (what the system is) through prospection (where it is tending) to controlled simulation (what it would do under pressure). ConstitutionsPlus is specified to be that instrument.

Four curated lines

What the platform reads.

Each line is a curated scientific script — a system with its own corpus, codebook and validation history — that the platform executes. Two exist; two are named and awaiting specification.

Line 1 · Exclusion

SOCE

Social-Ontological Cycle of Exclusion

Five nodes (inscription · ideology · imagined community · obedience · alterity), an eleven-link catalogue, a structural signature Φ = ⟨σ, V, E⟩, eleven historical cases in five topological families, descriptive and prospective modules (SOCE-D / SOCE-P).

Validated · codebook v0.1 deposited
Line 2 · Endurance

EMERGENZA

Constitutional endurance and replacement

Constitutions as transgenerational objects; survival analysis, contagion and agent models over the Comparative Constitutions Project record (1789–), with a transgenerational index calibrated on adaptability, flexibility and durability.

The in-emendable order

After Maurizio Ferraris

What in the order is unamendable-as-inscribed and what is amendable-as-interpreted; documentality as the mode in which constitutional facts acquire durability. To be specified as its own curated line.

Named · to be specified
Transversal axis — cooperation and predation. A common dynamic lexicon across the four lines: every coupling between actors, institutions and inscriptions is read as cooperative or predatory, which is what makes the lines comparable by link configuration rather than by ideological content.

On vocabulary: across the three sites, domain is reserved for the four social domains of the matrix (biological, economic, political, cultural). The four research lines above are therefore called lines here, so that a domain in the matrix and a line in the platform never collide.

Six constitutional functions

How the order is decomposed inside a line.

Within the constitutional region, the platform reads six coupled functions. They are not silos: legitimacy feeds membership, emergency rewrites sources and stresses endurance, and an exclusion closure can consolidate the "We" that re-inscribes the founding act. The object is the coupled network, not six separate analyses.

Method

Three layers on one object, governed by one constraint.

The simulation core turns each line's structural signature into an executable model. The methods are complementary layers, not alternatives; a transversal layer of nonlinear mathematics reads closure as a phase transition.

Agent-based modelling

Citizens, officials and factions act under adaptive rules; compliance modes, membership boundaries and institutional behaviour emerge from the bottom up. Natality enters as rule-innovation.

emergence · relations · agency

System dynamics

Stocks and flows of the order: reinforcing loops (closures), balancing loops (checks, endurance mechanisms), ratchets and hysteresis.

temporality · feedback

Network analysis

The coupled topology of functions and links; centrality, community structure, topological families — compared by link configuration, never by ideological content.

relational primacy

Nonlinear mathematics

Bifurcations and tipping points, path dependence, sensitivity to initial conditions — the early-warning reading of a loop that is about to shut.

transversal

Master constraint. If it cannot be read from a diagnosis, it does not enter the model. Resolution is declared and governed — ordinal and qualitative where the phenomenon is qualitative; no magnitude enters that a diagnosis cannot support. Reality, through retrodiction against known outcomes, is the judge.

Six design axes every model must declare

A1
Multi-level emergence and downward causation — micro-actors, meso-institutions, macro-order; the closure loop as the macro-bond that filters the next iteration.
A2
Ontological hardness vs interpretive amendability — what resists as inscribed, what is contested as interpreted; neither formalist rigidity nor constructivist dissolution.
A3
Level of abstraction — the modelling resolution is declared and fit for purpose; the master constraint lives here.
A4
Relational primacy — relations before nodes; the unit of analysis is the link configuration.
A5
Temporality and feedback — cyclical, durable and unpredictable time co-modelled as stocks, flows and loops; ratchets made explicit.
A6
Adaptation and agency — agents adapt rules under selection; the capacity to begin is never foreclosed; the seam where a governed intervention is still possible is located here.

Two results carry over from the validated lines as platform-wide expectations: multiple realisation (the same outcome on opposite bases) and intervention asymmetry (before closure one link may suffice; after closure only simultaneous multi-nodal intervention works) — the platform's most consequential practical output.

Visual evidence layer

Made legible, not persuasive.

The research question has three clauses: can exclusion be formalised as a cycle; does the cycle have reproducible topological properties; and can its detection be made legible for democratic practice? The visual layer is the instrument of the third clause. It is what turns a structural signature into something a jurist, a monitor or a citizen can read, check and disagree with.

Evidence, not advocacy

The layer follows evidence-presentation and visual-forensic practice — the tradition of Forensic Architecture — rather than data-storytelling. A picture here is a reconstruction: it shows what the coded record contains, in the order it was inscribed, and nothing the record does not support.

Symmetric structure

The notation has no majority baseline: the same rubrics apply whichever group is the "We" and whichever is the Other. The visual grammar is symmetric accordingly — the picture cannot be tilted by the ideological content of the case, only by its link configuration.

Per-element provenance

Every node, link and intensity drawn on screen carries its provenance: the source span, the coder or model that proposed it, the human who validated it, the codebook version, and its state — datum, interpretation, or a normative claim that the platform never draws. Legibility means being able to follow any element back to its inscription.

Legibility is a test, not a style

A reading counts as legible when practitioners in different institutions, working from the deposited pack, its visual layer and an open model stack, independently reproduce it. The picture is validated the same way the diagnosis is: by reproduction, not by approval.

What a signature looks like — schematic
Node size = ordinal intensity (L/P/D/E); line weight = link state (inactive · active · dominant · modulated · accelerating); the dashed teal edge is the recursive closure that turns episodic strain into a self-sustaining cycle. In the platform every element is clickable back to its provenance record. Schematic only — not a coded case.

The layer is not decoration on the platform; it is the platform's public face. The exploratory interface, the visualisation of coupled functions and the playable instrument are all built on it, and it serves all four lines with one grammar.

The two-tier rule

AI proposes, the human decides.

The rule is not a compliance slogan: it follows from the standpoint. The observer is an existing, plural being, and the capacity to begin something new is precisely what no simulation forecloses. That is the ontological reason the canonical verdict is always human-validated.

Exploratory tier — open

Anyone may run the published scripts, read cases, teach, probe the model, or play the interface. Every output is tagged with its state and its uncertainty; nothing here is a validated diagnosis.

Datum Interpretation Normative — never produced here

Canonical tier — governed

A diagnosis counts as canonical only when produced by trained human coders under the deposited protocol, with adjudication and a versioned codebook. Model outputs may propose values; only human coders validate them.

two coders · δ-protocol · versioned codebook · human verdict

White-box, model-agnostic. Input → process → output: information and method go in, agents curate the scientific literature by matching language hermeneutically without violating copyright, and usable results come out — reproducible by any group at near-zero compute cost. No fine-tuning, no vendor lock-in, no trackers on this site.
